# Contributing

Suggestions for new resources are welcome! Please read the criteria below before suggesting a link.

## Inclusion criteria

A resource is listed if it meets all of the following:

1. **About or in sign language.** Resources primarily about sign language or Deaf culture, or primarily presented in sign language. General sites with only incidental sign language content are not included.
2. **Reference, educational, or media content.** Dictionaries, courses, corpora, and research, as well as media produced in sign language (TV, film, news, performance), including resources run by individuals. Personal blogs, vlogs, social media accounts, and opinion pieces are not included.
3. **Free to use.** The core content is free, with no paywall. A free sign-up is fine; one that requires payment details or institutional access is not. Optional paid features and ads are acceptable, but not sites mainly selling courses or services.
4. **Substantial and functional.** It has enough content to be genuinely useful for its purpose now. Small single-purpose tools qualify if they do their job fully. No placeholders, "coming soon" pages, or largely empty projects.
5. **Reliable.** The site loads and its main features work. 

Resources may be in any language.

Resources that stop meeting these criteria, including links that stay broken, will be removed.

## Suggesting or reporting a resource

Fill in [this form](https://forms.gle/qR2L4hmBKVZ6utrB7) (no GitHub account needed) or [open a new issue](https://github.com/isaranga/Sign-Language-Resources/issues/new) to suggest a resource, or to report one that is broken or no longer meets the criteria.

When suggesting through an issue, include:
* the link
* which sign language it covers (or "general")
* a short description of what it offers
* the language of the site, if not English
* whether significant features require payment or sign-up
